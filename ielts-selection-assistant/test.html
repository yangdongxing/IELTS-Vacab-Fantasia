测试段落 2：旅行与时态变形测试（含已有加粗文本测试）

Our pace was slow because the river frequently had sharp bends through deep valleys. When we reached the valley, it was quiet and the columns of ice hung from the rocks. She disagreed with taking the train and preferred cycling.
